About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and an incoming Postdoctoral Associate at Cornell University, where I will work with Prof. Fengqi You.

My work centers on optimization, prediction, forecasting, and system modeling for applied problems, with an emphasis on reliable and interpretable workflows.
